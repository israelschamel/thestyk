Find your stuff with a sticker.

Styk is a Bluetooth tracker you peel and stick onto a notebook, laptop, bike or wallet. It's about as thin as a few sheets of card, with a small solar cell to help keep its battery topped up.

Get launch updates

Why Styk

Ultra-thin

Designed as a flat sticker rather than a puck, so it fits where other trackers can't: inside covers, on laptop lids, under labels.

Solar-assisted

A built-in solar film harvests room light and sunlight to help recharge the battery, so you should rarely have to think about it.

Make it ring

Can't find it? Tap a button in the app and the built-in buzzer sounds off.

How it works

1

Stick it on

Peel the backing and press it onto something you don't want to lose.

2

Your phone listens

Styk sends a small Bluetooth signal. When your phone is nearby it sees the tag and records where it last saw it.

3

Find it

Open the app to see signal strength, battery and "last seen", then play a sound to pinpoint it.

Styk has no GPS of its own. Location always comes from a phone that is near it.

Built with privacy in mind

Rotating identifiers

The tag changes the ID it broadcasts regularly, so it can't be followed by a fixed signal.

Unknown-tracker alerts

Designed so that a tag traveling with someone who isn't its owner can make a sound and be detected.

You're in control

Pairing is yours to approve, and you can remove a tag from the app at any time.
